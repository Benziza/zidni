The idea, simply put

Discover without searching.

Zidni is a free tool for discovering random Wikipedia articles. Each click offers a new page, an excerpt and a link to the full article. Articles are available in French, English, Arabic and Moroccan Darija.

How do I keep an article?

The “Keep” button adds a page to your notebook. You can find it again, search for a word or export your reading list as a text file.

Where is my notebook stored?

In this browser, on this device. No account is needed. Clearing browser data also clears the notebook; export it to keep a copy.

Where do the articles come from?

Excerpts are loaded from the Wikipedia API. Text belongs to its contributors and is offered under the CC BY-SA licence. Visit the source page for the full information and any image credits.

How do I discover another page?

Swipe upward on the card, press the up arrow while the card is focused, or use Next discovery. Read more opens the full article on Wikipedia.
